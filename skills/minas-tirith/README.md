## 🏰 The White City

Minas Tirith — "Tower of Guard" — was the capital of Gondor in Tolkien's legendarium. A seven-walled city carved into the mountainside, each level more refined than the last. Its white stone gleamed in the sun, earning it the name "the White City."

In The Lord of the Skills, Minas Tirith represents **UI & Design** — the craft of making every surface intentional, every transition purposeful, every color deliberate. Just as the stonemasons of Minas Tirith shaped their city with care, the skills in this kingdom help your agent craft interfaces with taste.
